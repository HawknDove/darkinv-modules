KEY CONCEPT

What IS The Engine?

THE KID VERSION "The Engine is your personal trade signal machine. It takes everything we've learned — candles, structure, momentum, indicators — and puts it all through a filter. When everything lines up, The Engine lights up. GREEN = go, RED = stop, YELLOW = wait. It doesn't replace your judgment — it CONFIRMS it."

GREEN

All conditions align for a long. Buyers in control, momentum up. Look for entry, stop below recent low, target at next resistance.

RED

All conditions align for a short. Sellers in control, momentum down. Look for short entry, stop above recent high, target at next support.

YELLOW

Mixed signals. Market undecided. Not enough edge. STAY OUT — patience IS a position.

NEVER fight the Engine signal.

The Engine + Price Action

◈ COMPANION · 3ATM BUNDLE

When All Three Agree — That's Your Signal

The 3ATM Bundle is the engine's highest-confidence signal. When MACD, ADX, and RSI all align at the same time — that's not luck, that's a high-probability setup calling your name.
① MACD
What it does: Measures momentum. When the MACD line crosses above the signal line — bulls are in control. Below — bears are in control.

READY when: MACD is above the signal line (call) or below (put) and in the right territory.
② ADX
What it does: Measures TREND STRENGTH — not direction, just how strong the move is. Below 15? No trend. Above 20 and rising? Trend confirmed.

READY when: ADX is above threshold AND actively rising.
③ RSI
What it does: Measures momentum on a 0–100 scale. Above 50 favors bulls. Below 50 favors bears. Above 70 = overbought. Below 30 = oversold.

READY when: RSI above 47 for calls · below 47 for puts.
1Watch the scorecard ◈ 3ATM row. It counts: 1/3 → 2/3 → READY
2When it shows READY in green — all three aligned for a CALL setup
3When it shows READY in red — all three aligned for a PUT setup
43ATM READY + Zone confirmation = highest probability entry. Wait for both.
5Never force a trade when it shows 1/3 or 2/3. Patience is part of the strategy.
"The 3ATM bundle doesn't fire often — but when it does, it fires with conviction. Your job is to be ready and disciplined enough to wait for it." — Coach K
◈ COMPANION · ENTRY & EXIT MASTERY

Getting In Right. Getting Out Smart.

Most beginners focus only on entries. Professionals obsess over exits. Your exit is where your profit is protected or lost. Know both before you touch the keyboard.
🟢 ENTRY — When To Get In
ANCHOR windows: 0, 1, 8 minutes of each 10-min cycle — highest probability entry times

3ATM READY + Zone confirmation + session active = ▲ FIRE or ▼ FIRE signal

Volume SURGE confirms the move has institutional backing
🟠 EXIT — When To Get Out
Orange circle on chart = engine says exit now

Chandelier Exit: price drops 2× ATR from the high water mark

Breakeven: once up 10 pts, stop moves to entry — you can't lose

EXIT ZONE windows: 0, 3, 7 minutes — watch closely
1Set your Stop Loss FIRST — below the recent low (long) or above recent high (short). Always before entry.
2Take Profit target — minimum 2:1 reward-to-risk. Risk 25 pts → target 60 pts.
3Trail activates at 5 pts profit — the engine starts trailing your stop automatically
4Yellow candle appears during a trade — warning. Don't add. Watch closely. Engine may exit.
5White triangle appears = SL/TP/Trail hit. Orange circle = engine exited. Know your markers.
"Amateurs think about entries. Professionals think about exits. Know exactly where you're getting out BEFORE you get in." — Coach K
