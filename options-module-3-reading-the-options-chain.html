KEY CONCEPT

What Is an Options Chain?

An options chain is a table showing every available option contract for a stock — every strike price and every expiration date, for both calls and puts, side by side.

THE KID VERSION "Think of it like a menu at a restaurant, except instead of food prices, it lists every possible 'reservation price' (strike) you could lock in, and how much each reservation (premium) costs today. CALLS sit on the left, PUTS sit on the right, and strikes run down the middle — same layout every time, on every stock."

Strike Price

The price the option holder can buy (call) or sell (put) the stock at. Here, 453 is a projected share price level.

The date the contract expires. Here, Dec 4th 2023 is when this specific row's options expire.

Premium (Mark)

The price paid for the contract. Always multiply by 100 — .335 × 100 = $33.50 for one contract.
