THIS IS THE MOMENT

The Complete Trade Setup — 11 Steps

Tonight we execute everything we've learned. LIVE. Every step called out loud. This is not theory anymore — this is how you will trade for the rest of your career.
1Check daily chart — what's the trend? Above or below 200MA? Where is major S/R?
2Check the Engine — Green, Red, or Yellow? If Yellow, stop here and wait.
3Confirm structure — clear support (long) or resistance (short)?
4Wait for signal candle — Hammer at support (long), Shooting Star at resistance (short).
5Check indicators — do at least 2 (RSI, MACD, Stoch RSI, DMI) agree with the Engine?
6Set your stop loss — below recent low (long) or above recent high (short). Place it FIRST.
7Set your target — minimum 2:1 reward-to-risk, at next major S/R.
8Calculate position size — how many contracts keeps risk under 2%?
9Enter the trade — candle has closed, checklist complete. Execute.
10Manage the trade — watch but don't touch. No moving stops against you.
11Journal the trade — screenshot, entry, exit, P&L, and what you felt. Win or loss.
