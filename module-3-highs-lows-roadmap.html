KEY CONCEPT

Market Structure — The Staircase

THE KID VERSION "The market moves like stairs. Uptrend = stairs going UP (each step is higher than the last). Downtrend = stairs going DOWN (each step is lower). The trick is knowing which staircase you're on BEFORE you start climbing."

Uptrend — Stairs Going UP

  • Higher High (HH): each new peak is above the last peak
  • Higher Low (HL): each pullback stops higher than the last
  • Trend is UP as long as HH + HL holds
  • One Lower Low breaks the structure — stay alert
  • Best trades: buy the Higher Low (the "dip")

Downtrend — Stairs Going DOWN

  • Lower High (LH): each bounce gets rejected lower
  • Lower Low (LL): each drop goes further down
  • Trend is DOWN as long as LH + LL holds
  • One Higher High can signal a reversal
  • Best trades: short the Lower High (the "bounce")
KEY CONCEPT

Support & Resistance — The Floor and Ceiling

THE KID VERSION "Support is like a TRAMPOLINE under price — it bounces. Resistance is like a CEILING above price — it blocks. The more times price bounces off a level, the STRONGER that level is. When the floor breaks, it becomes the new ceiling. When the ceiling breaks, it becomes the new floor."

Support — The Floor

  • A price floor where buyers step in
  • More bounces = stronger support
  • When support breaks, it becomes new resistance

Resistance — The Ceiling

  • A price ceiling where sellers take over
  • More rejections = stronger resistance
  • When resistance breaks, it becomes new support
Break of Structure (BOS): bullish BOS = price breaks above a previous high, old resistance flips to support. Bearish BOS = price breaks below a previous low, old support flips to resistance. Confirmation: close above/below the level on the daily or 15-min chart.
KNOW YOUR TOOLS

The 7 Indicators

Indicators are tools, not crystal balls. Your job is to stack multiple indicators so they agree before you pull the trigger. When 4–5 point the same direction, that's a high-probability trade.

ROC — Rate of Change

Measures how fast price is moving vs N periods ago. Rising above zero = accelerating up. Falling below zero = accelerating down.

"It's a speedometer — shows HOW FAST you're getting there, not just where."

HMA — Hull Moving Average

A smoothed moving average with much less lag. Rising + price above = uptrend confirmed. Steep slope = strong momentum.

"Regular MAs text you 10 minutes late. HMA texts you instantly."

200-Day Moving Average

THE BIG ONE — every institution watches this line. Price above = long-term bull. Price below = long-term bear.

"When price crosses it, the whole market changes its opinion."

Stochastic RSI

Below 20 = oversold, potential bounce. Above 80 = overbought. %K crossing %D from below 20 = buy signal.

"A check on a check — is RSI itself tired?"

DMI / ADX

+DI = bull strength, -DI = bear strength. ADX above 25 = strong trend. Below 20 = choppy — avoid trend trades.

"A tug-of-war scoreboard — ADX tells you how hard both sides are pulling."

MACD

12 EMA vs 26 EMA. MACD crossing above signal = bullish. Crossing below = bearish. Histogram size = momentum strength.

"Two runners racing — the histogram shows how far ahead they are."

RSI

0–100 scale. Above 70 = overbought. Below 30 = oversold. Divergence from price = powerful reversal signal.

"A rubber band pulled too far wants to snap back — but it can stay stretched a while in a trend."

◈ COMPANION · SUPPLY & DEMAND ZONES

Where the Big Money Buyers and Sellers Live

Support and Resistance are lines on a chart. Supply and Demand ZONES are areas where institutional money made big decisions. Price always comes back to these zones — that's your edge.
🟢 DEMAND ZONE
Area where buyers overwhelmed sellers. Price shot UP from here before. When price RETURNS to this zone — buyers step in again.

What to do: Look for CALL signals, 3ATM READY, green candle confirmation.
🔴 SUPPLY ZONE
Area where sellers overwhelmed buyers. Price dropped HARD from here before. When price RETURNS to this zone — sellers step in again.

What to do: Look for PUT signals, 3ATM READY, red candle confirmation.
1Breakout: Price breaks ABOVE a supply zone with surge + momentum = aggressive call entry signal
2Retest: Price breaks above, pulls back to test the old zone as new support, bounces = conservative entry
3The Engine scorecard Zone row tells you: DEMAND · SUPPLY · BREAK UP · BREAK DN · RETEST
4Gray zone on chart = inactive. Green zone = active demand. Red zone = active supply
5Highest probability: 3ATM READY + price IN a demand zone = FIRE signal. Two confirmations, one entry.
"Support and resistance are floors and ceilings. Supply and demand zones are rooms where decisions were made. Learn to see the rooms." — Coach K
