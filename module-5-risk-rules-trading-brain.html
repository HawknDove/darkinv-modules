Week 3 · Session 5 — Risk Management & Psychology

KEY CONCEPT

Risk Management — The Non-Negotiables

THE KID VERSION "Imagine you have a sandwich for lunch every day. Risk management means you NEVER bet your whole sandwich on one trade. You only bet a tiny bite. That way, even if you lose — you still have your sandwich. Most traders blow up because they bet the whole sandwich. DON'T."

The 1-2% Rule

Never risk more than 1-2% of your account on ANY single trade. $5,000 account = max $50–$100 risk per trade.

Stop Loss Required

Goes on the chart BEFORE you enter. Not after. No exceptions. It is your seatbelt.

1:2 Risk-Reward Minimum

If you risk $50, your target must be at least $100. Never take a trade where you risk more than you can gain.

Position Sizing

If your stop is far, use fewer contracts. If stop is tight, you can use more.

Daily Loss Limit

Set a daily max loss (e.g. 3% of account). When you hit it — STOP. Log off. Tomorrow is a new day.

Max Trades Per Day

Beginners: max 3 trades per day. Quality over quantity. Overtrading is the #1 killer of accounts.

KNOW YOUR ENEMIES

The Trading Brain

The market is designed to trigger your emotions. Every dip is designed to scare you out. Every spike is designed to pull you in too late. The professional trader's ONLY advantage is emotional control and a proven process.

Fear

Exiting profitable trades too early. Not entering valid setups. Moving stop loss to break-even too fast.

Antidote: trust the process. Define your exit before you enter.

Greed

Holding past your target. Adding more contracts after winners. Ignoring stop losses.

Antidote: lock in partial profits. Scale OUT, not up, after wins.

Revenge Trading

Jumping right back in after a loss to "make it back." Larger size after losses. Ignoring rules.

Antidote: log off for 30 minutes minimum after a loss. Journal first.

FOMO

Chasing a move after it already happened. Entering without confirmation because "it's going!"

Antidote: "There is always another trade." Missing one setup beats a bad trade.

Overconfidence

After 3 winners in a row — taking sloppy trades, skipping checklist, sizing up too fast.

Antidote: your best trading comes from the process, not your feelings. Stay humble after wins.
BUILD THE HABIT

The Daily Trading Routine

30 MIN BEFORE OPENCheck daily chart, mark S/R levels, note economic events, check 200MA position — know the battlefield before the battle.
AT MARKET OPENWatch the first 15–30 minutes. Do NOT trade the open as a beginner — the open is the most manipulated time.
DURING SESSIONRun the checklist. Wait for Engine + candle + structure alignment. Max 3 trades — quality over quantity.
AFTER SESSIONJournal every trade, win or lose. Screenshot the chart. Note your emotions — champions watch the film.
EVENING20 minutes of chart study. Find patterns. No trading — just building pattern recognition.
"The market tests your character before it tests your chart reading."
◈ COMPANION · TRADING PSYCHOLOGY

The Game Inside the Game

You can know every indicator, every pattern, every rule — and still blow your account if your mind isn't right. Trading psychology is not optional. It IS the strategy.
😱
FEAR
Stops you from taking valid setups. Makes you exit too early. Leads to missed profits.
🤑
GREED
Keeps you in too long. Makes you move your take profit. Turns winners into losers.
😤
REVENGE
Trading to get back a loss. FOMO after a missed entry. The fastest way to blow an account.
🧘
DISCIPLINE
Follows the rules when it's hard. Sits on hands when there's no signal. Trusts the engine.
