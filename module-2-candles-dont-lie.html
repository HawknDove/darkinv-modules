KEY CONCEPT

The 4 Parts of Every Candle

THE KID VERSION "A candle is like a 5-minute arm wrestling match. The OPEN is when they grab hands. The CLOSE is when someone wins. The HIGH is how far back the winner pushed. The LOW is how far the loser was pushed before fighting back. The WICKS are the battle scars — proof someone tried and got rejected."

HIGHthe ceiling they hit

OPENthe starting gun

CLOSEthe final score

LOWthe floor they found

WICKthe war-zone lines — rejected price

THE 6 TYPES YOU MUST KNOW

Every candle tells one of six stories

Bullish

Close > Open. Buyers won. Bigger body = stronger move.

Bearish

Close < Open. Sellers won. Be careful buying into a string of these.

Doji

Open ≈ Close. Neither side won. Never trade into one.

Hammer

Long lower wick. Buyers fought back hard. Possible reversal UP.

Shooting Star

Long upper wick. Sellers rejected the push up. Watch at resistance.

Spinning Top

Small body, wicks both sides. Market pausing — wait it out.

⚠️ Mistake to avoid: don't chase a 5th green candle in a row — a pullback is often coming. And never trade into a doji; wait for the next candle to show direction.
