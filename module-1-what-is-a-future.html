What Is a Future?

Week 1 · Session 1 — "Taught like you're 5. Traded like a pro."

MEET THE IDEA

A future is a promise you make today

Imagine you tell your friend: "I promise to trade you my toy car for $10 next Friday — no matter what." Even if your car gets a scratch, even if your friend changes their mind, you both agreed on the price today for something that happens later.

That's a futures contract. Traders aren't buying the actual thing right now — they're agreeing on a price for a set moment in the future. As day traders, we make that promise and then let it go before the day ends, trying to catch the price moving up or down along the way.

🌈 Rainbow Engine version: a future is a promise on a price. Our job is to read where that price is likely headed before everyone else sees it.
TWO WAYS TO WIN

Long vs. Short

🟢 LONG = you bought first. You want the price to go UP so you can sell higher later.

🔴 SHORT = you sold first. You want the price to go DOWN so you can buy it back cheaper later.

The exciting part: in futures, you can try to win either way — up or down — as long as you read the market right.

MEET THE FUTURES FAMILY

Tick & point values — the numbers that matter

Every time price moves the smallest possible step, that's called a tick. Each symbol has its own dollar value per tick and per point (4 ticks = 1 point on NQ/ES/MNQ/MES; on YM/MYM, 1 tick = 1 point).

NQ

E-mini Nasdaq-100 · "The Big Dog"
$5.00 per tick
$20.00 per point
Fast · Tech-heavy · Volatile

MNQ

Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100
$0.50 per tick
$2.00 per point
Where beginners START

ES

E-mini S&P 500 · Institutional standard
$12.50 per tick
$50.00 per point
Calmer · Highest global volume

MES

Micro E-mini S&P 500
$1.25 per tick
$5.00 per point
Small size, same chart

YM

E-mini Dow Jones
$5.00 per tick (= 1 point)
1 tick = 1 point — simple math

MYM

Micro E-mini Dow Jones
$0.50 per tick (= 1 point)
1/10th the size of YM
🌈 Rainbow Engine rule: always know your dollars-per-tick BEFORE you click buy or sell. It's the number that controls how big your stop can safely be.
